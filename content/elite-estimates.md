# Elite Pharmaceuticals — meaningful estimates (three claim tiers)

Source: Elite 10-K, FY ended 3/31/2026 (SEC accession 0001493152-26-031070).
Purpose: give the named case real numbers WITHOUT attributing unapproved results.

## Tier A — public facts (usable anywhere today, cite the 10-K)

- Net revenue **$148.9M FY2026**, up **77%** from $84.0M (FY25); $56.6M FY24 — 2.6× in two years
- **Indirect wholesaler sales — the chargeback-bearing flow — grew 2.4× in one year**: $34.0M → $82.5M
- Revenue is booked **net of chargebacks, discounts and program rebates** (their words); the 10-K defines "allocated revenues" as gross **less chargebacks and government rebates processed** — chargeback processing is structural to the business
- **Two wholesale customers = 74% of revenue** (one = 64%) — concentration that makes every claim dispute matter
- **65 full-time employees** run all of it — a $149M company with a lean back office
- Accounts receivable doubled ($29.2M → $59.7M) alongside the volume surge

The narrative: *"Elite's chargeback-bearing volume grew 2.4× in a single year. That is the
volume the Settle lever had to absorb — validated before payment, not after."*
