# The Leak Ledger Method — Sales One-Pager (internal)

**The line:** *"The Leak Ledger Method: we find the leaks across your Quote-to-Comply
pipeline — four questions, your numbers — then close them, lever by lever."*

**The funnel you're selling:** Engine (minutes, self-serve) → Leak Ledger Workshop
(one day, their data) → lever engagements (fixed-fee, one quarter each).
Everything below exists to earn the workshop.

---

## The grid: four questions × three motions

| # | The invariant question | Direct (Mfg CTO) | Channel (Semi · Pharma) | Recurring (SaaS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What price was promised?** | Quote | Agree — contract · GPO tier · design registration | Quote → termed contract |
| 2 | **What actually transacted?** | Order | Transact — disti PO · POS · EDI 867 | Contract — provisioning + every billing cycle |
| 3 | **What was settled & recognized?** | Recognize — billing, rev rec | Settle — chargebacks · S&D credits · rebates | Recur — renewals, uplift, ratable 606 |
| 4 | **What was filed on it?** | Comply — sales/use tax | Comply — post-claim basis · gov pricing | Comply — multi-state SaaS tax |

Ask the four questions in order, in the prospect's vocabulary. You are not pitching;
you are auditing out loud. The method IS the demo.

## Discovery questions per lever (pick the motion's column)

**Q1 — the price promise**
- Direct: "Who builds a complex quote today, and how long does it take? What happens when they're out?"
- Channel: "When a design registration lapses or a contract tier is misapplied — who notices, and when?"
- SaaS: "Does the signed contract ever differ from the quote? Who reconciles them?"

**Q2 — the transaction**
- Direct: "Does anyone re-key the quote into the ERP? What % of orders need correction?"
- Channel: "How does POS/EDI data arrive, and who matches it to agreements?"
- SaaS: "Has provisioning or the first invoice ever not matched the contract?"

**Q3 — settlement & recognition** *(the crown jewel — slow down here)*
- Direct: "How long is month-end close? What's rebuilt in Excel every time?"
- Channel: "Before AP pays a claim, what is it validated against?" *(silence here = the deal)*
- SaaS: "How is renewal uplift applied — by system or by someone remembering?"

**Q4 — the filing**
- All: "When claims/credits/renewals change the numbers, does tax find out?"

## Objections

- **"Our ERP/CPQ already does this."** "It executes the happy path. The ledger lives in the
  handoffs — show me the three-way match on last month's claims and I'll leave happy."
- **"We'd need IT for months."** "The engine is live in minutes from Claude — the workshop
  needs a CSV export, not a project."
- **"Your numbers are generic."** "Correct — they're benchmarks. That's the point of the
  workshop: one day, your data, your ledger. If it comes up empty, you got a free audit."
- **"AI pricing our quotes? No."** "Agents talk. The engine decides. Every number comes from
  your rules — the AI never invents a price. That's the whole product."
- **"We're channel — we don't quote."** "Exactly — your price lives in agreements and leaks
  through claims. Same four questions, your vocabulary." *(show Agree→Transact→Settle→Comply)*
