## The mental model
Classic SEO ranks pages; AI-era SEO (GEO/AEO) gets you **cited and recommended by answer
engines** (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). Answer engines favor:
clear entities, quotable stats with sources, question-shaped content, review aggregators,
and — uniquely for Nteli — **being INSIDE the AI as a connector**.
