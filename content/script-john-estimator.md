## Render notes
- **The tagline is the last thing seen and heard.** Hold the closing card a full 4 seconds — it's also the freeze-frame most social players will show.
- Mid-film plant deliberately personalizes it ("**John** talks. The engine decides.") so the closing generic form ("Agents talk.") reads as the universal rule John's story proved.
- Persistent ledger strip (from 0:10) is the formula made visible: the open promises outcomes, the film ticks them off on screen, the close totals them.
- Queue card (0:28) mirrors the real MCP Apps card — counts, red overdue badge, `Open in Nteli ↗` — matching what a trial user sees in Claude.
- Compatibility block reuses the corrected hazloc language ("listed for the location") — never UL 698A.
- "$1.4M / $200M" derivation: $80M quoted-config revenue × 3% config/discount error × 60% unrecoverable (Leak Ledger EP1 math block).
- John is a fictional composite estimator; no real customer named.
- Thumbnail subline for this video (and the next thumbnail batch): **"An AI estimator on a checked quoting engine."** — replaces the retired forward-deployed line; the video tagline stays "Agents talk. The engine decides."
