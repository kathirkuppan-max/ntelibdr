quoted by conversation.

Say “1 MW, two rows of 20 racks at 25 kW, N+1” — the engine sizes the racks, containment, power train and cooling together and prices the pod as one BOM. Every number comes from your rules — never from a guess.

Racks, containment, power train and cooling sized together: kW per rack drives PDUs, UPS under N+1 or 2N, generators, CRAH and CDU.

What it sizes together

  • →kW per rack drives the rack PDUs and feeds — and everything upstream of them.
  • →UPS modules and battery runtime sized under the redundancy you choose — N, N+1 or 2N — with step-down transformers per side.
  • →Generators, switchgear and transfer gear sized to the pod’s load, with interrupting ratings checked.
  • →Cooling — CRAH, coolant distribution, rear-door heat exchangers — checked against the heat each rack actually sheds, so a 40 kW rack never ships with 25 kW of cooling.
  • →Containment, row busway and commissioning levels carried into the same BOM.
  • →Every size and every refusal shows its source — the rule, the rating, the catalog page.
