Practice Areas
What We Do

Four practices. One channel revenue problem.

Every industry that sells through a channel faces the same root problem: the data that drives claims, recognition, compliance, and tax lives in three systems that don't talk to each other. We build the bridge.

Semiconductor & Electronics
Ship & Debit adjudication, Design Registration lifecycle, POS reconciliation, and GL-grade accounting for fabless semi, power electronics, and component manufacturers. Multi-tier channel programs: distributor → rep → end customer.
Ship & Debit · Design Reg · POS Rec · ASC 606
Pharma & Life Sciences
340B compliance, chargeback adjudication, GPO contract management, and EDI 844/867/849 processing. Every claim traced to a contract before it hits AP. HRSA OPAIS sync, WAC/contract price differential automation.
340B · Chargebacks · GPO · EDI 844/867/849
SaaS & Subscription / Q2C
Quote-to-cash on any stack — NetSuite, SAP, HubSpot, Dynamics 365, Salesforce, or fully custom. CPQ configuration, subscription lifecycle, ASC 606 revenue recognition, partner channel programs, and AR controls. Platform follows the problem.
CPQ · Revenue Cloud · ASC 606 · Partner Programs
Tax Compliance / Avalara
AvaTax implementation across CRM and ERP stacks, CertCapture for exemption certificate management, channel price adjustment tax logic (S&D claims → AvaTax), and Avalara Returns for multi-state automated filing. The only Avalara partner that owns the channel data too.
AvaTax · CertCapture · Multi-state Filing · Returns
