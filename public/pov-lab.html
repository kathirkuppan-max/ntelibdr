Point of view: built on Salesforce · For Salesforce sellers of manufacturing products · Lab equipment

Lab equipment sold through dealers, quoted right and traced to the order, on Salesforce.

For lab equipment makers with thousands of products, custom builds and a few dealers carrying most of the revenue. Reps find any product by what it is, not by its product ID. Custom builds are configured, not built in Excel. Every dealer order is created from the quote it answers.

The whole story, in the same words every time

    What your prospect would get

    • A catalog reps search by what the product is: type, size, class. Nobody memorizes product IDs.
    • Custom builds configured with their limits, instead of built in Excel and added by hand.
    • Checks that stop what can’t be built or installed, before any price goes out to the dealer.
    • Every dealer order created from the quote it answers, so orders trace back to their quotes.
    • Sales Agreements and the Account Forecast for the dealers that carry most of the revenue.
    • The entire rule pack, pricing and dashboards pre-built. Agentforce proposes; the rep approves.
