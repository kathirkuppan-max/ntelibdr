UL 508A control panels — breaker schedules, enclosure ratings, and heat loads checked before pricing.

What it checks

  • →Supply voltage — 208, 240, 480 or 600 V — asked when the RFQ doesn't say.
  • →Motor current from NEC Table 430.250; the main sized to NEC 430.24 / 409.20.
  • →SCCR against the available fault current, and the main breaker's interrupting rating at the supply voltage.
  • →Starters — full-voltage, reversing, soft — and drives from 1 to 50 hp, sized from published manufacturer ratings.
  • →Enclosure type — NEMA 1, 12, 3R, 4, 4X — and cooling: no fans on 3R, 4 or 4X.
  • →Every size and every refusal shows its source: the code section or the catalog page.
