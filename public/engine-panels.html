A complete quote is not a correct one.

A price-book quoting tool makes sure nothing is left off: the ferrules, the duct, the label, the crate. That matters. But it can’t tell you the panel is wrong: starters rated 5 kA on a 22 kA service, a main sized from the wrong motor currents, a filtered fan on a NEMA 4X door. Those mistakes cost more than a missing line, and they surface at inspection, after the PO.

Nteli checks both. It prices the whole panel from your price book, and it checks the panel against UL 508A and the NEC before the price goes out, with the code section or catalog page behind every size and every refusal.

UL 508A quoting: questions panel shops ask

What is UL 508A control panel quoting software?

Software that turns a panel request into a priced quote for an industrial control panel built to UL 508A. Most tools make sure nothing is left off the quote. Nteli also checks that the panel is right: motor currents from NEC Table 430.250, the main sized to NEC 430.24 and 409.20, SCCR against the available fault current, and enclosure cooling rules, before a price goes out.

Does it check the panel's SCCR?

Yes. It compares each starter's and drive's short-circuit rating with the available fault current and the main breaker's interrupting rating at the supply voltage. At 22 kA, for example, standard 5 kA starters get Class J/CC fuse kits and the main moves to a high-interrupting breaker. If the fault current isn't in the RFQ, it prices the panel and holds the quote for that site fact instead of guessing.

Can it use my own price book?

Yes. Prices are illustrative until your price book loads; after that every line is priced from it, and your own rules can override the starter rule pack, governed and audited.

Does it replace EPLAN or AutoCAD Electrical?

No. It works before design: from the RFQ, a device list or the loads. The same quote produces the submittal sheet your customer approves before the PO, with ratings, a dimensioned layout, a one-line diagram and the bill of materials. Detailed design stays in your CAD tool.

What about switchgear, MCCs and hazardous locations?

Switchgear and MCC-sized jobs go to your engineers for review, with the details already gathered. Panels for Class/Division or Zone locations are quoted on the hazardous-area line.
