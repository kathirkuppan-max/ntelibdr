The Problem

Your partners aren't using the portal. Because it doesn't work for them.

A partner portal that's hard to use is worse than no portal — partners learn to work around it, and you lose visibility into everything they're doing. Experience Cloud is powerful, but it requires implementation that actually maps to how distributors and resellers operate.

Without a working portal

✕
Deal registration happens via email — half of deals never get registered, and you have no pipeline visibility into what your partners are working
✕
MDF requests go into a shared inbox — approved (or not) inconsistently, with no audit trail, no proof-of-performance, and no closed-loop to CRM
✕
Partners call your inside sales team for pricing — everyone's time wasted, your reps interrupted, and the partner still doesn't close the deal faster
✕
Training materials are in a SharePoint folder nobody can find — certifications aren't tracked, and your top-tier partners don't get the enablement they need
✕
Partner tier reviews require someone to manually compile spreadsheets — quarterly, painful, and always delayed by the time channel ops gets the data

With Nteli Experience Cloud

✓
Partners register deals in 60 seconds from their phone — conflict resolution rules fire automatically, approval workflows route in real time, and the opp lands in your CRM pipeline
✓
MDF requests routed automatically — approved against budget, proof-of-performance tracked, and payout integrated — end-to-end visibility from request to reimbursement
✓
Partners find pricing, product info, and quote tools without calling anyone — live price book exposed through the portal, gated by tier and territory
✓
