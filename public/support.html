Common questions

Where are the product demos?

nteli.io/engine — thirteen two-minute demos, one per vertical. Deep links like /engine/hvac open a specific vertical.

How do I connect it to Claude?

The engine is an MCP server. Connection is via OAuth when your workspace is set up — see the documentation, and email us if your MCP client needs anything unusual.

How does pricing work?

By credits — one credit per submitted quote; the configuration conversation is free. Details on the pricing page and in the documentation.

When can I sign up?

Self-serve sign-up is launching soon. Email us to be first in line, or book a call for a guided start.

Something looks wrong on a quote

Every number traces to a rule. Ask the engine "why?" first — then email us with the quote ID if the rule itself needs attention. Rule fixes go through your workspace's approval flow.

Data export or deletion

Workspace admins can email hello@nteli.io to request export or deletion — see the privacy policy.
