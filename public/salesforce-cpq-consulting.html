FAQ

Is Salesforce CPQ being discontinued?

Salesforce CPQ is end of sale for new customers, not end of life. Existing customers can renew, add users and get support, and there’s no announced retirement date. New implementations go on Revenue Cloud. Our CPQ end-of-sale guide covers the details.

Should a new implementation use Salesforce CPQ or Revenue Cloud?

Revenue Cloud. It’s where Salesforce is investing, and new customers can no longer buy CPQ. If you already run CPQ, we can improve it now and plan the move to Revenue Cloud for when it makes business sense.

Can you fix our existing Salesforce CPQ?

Yes. A CPQ health check reviews product rules, price rules, discount schedules, quote templates and performance, then we fix what slows reps down or produces wrong quotes.

How long does a Salesforce CPQ implementation take?

A focused first phase, one product line with its pricing and quote document, can go live in weeks. Complex configured products, many price lists or ERP integration add scope.

Do you work with Conga and DocuSign?

Yes. We implement Conga for quote documents and contract lifecycle management, and connect e-signature into the quote process.

Do you work outside Texas?

Yes. We’re based in Austin and deliver CPQ projects remotely across the United States and Canada.
