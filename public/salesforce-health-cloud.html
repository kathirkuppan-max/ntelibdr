FAQ

What is Salesforce Health Cloud?

Salesforce’s CRM for healthcare: a patient and member view, care coordination, referrals and outreach, built on the Salesforce platform with healthcare data models.

Is Salesforce HIPAA compliant?

Salesforce signs a business associate agreement (BAA) for covered services, and compliance also depends on how the org is configured: access, encryption, audit and what data goes where. We design for HIPAA from the start and work under a BAA.

Can Salesforce Health Cloud integrate with Cerner?

Yes. We integrate Salesforce with EHR and EMR systems, including Cerner (now Oracle Health), typically through MuleSoft and the EHR’s HL7 or FHIR interfaces. The same patterns apply to other EHRs.

Which hospitals have you worked with?

Children’s Mercy Kansas City, where we provided Health Cloud and Marketing Cloud managed services, and Hoag in California, on a HIPAA-compliant Health Cloud implementation.

Do you provide Health Cloud managed services?

Yes: day-to-day administration, break/fix, enhancements and in-flight projects, as we did for Children’s Mercy Kansas City.

Do you work with life sciences companies too?

Yes. In life sciences we’ve delivered Salesforce for pharma (Elite Pharmaceuticals) and medical device manufacturers (Quasar, Intricon).
