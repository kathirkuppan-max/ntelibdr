Fair questions.

Why quotes, not seats?

Seat pricing punishes you for letting more people use the engine. We want your reps, reviewers, and engineers all in it — the unit that tracks value is the quote that goes out the door.

What if a quote isn't approved?

The credit covers submission — the approval routing, review packet, and audit trail around it. Rejected and re-worked quotes in the same session don't spend another credit.

Do unused credits roll over?

Credits reset monthly on Starter and Team. Scale plans can bank up to one month of unused credits. Forward-Deployed credit pools are sized annually to your real volume.

Do rule changes cost anything?

Never. Teaching the engine — proposing a rule, previewing its impact on history, peer approval — is governance, not consumption. It's included in every tier.

What counts as a product model?

One rule pack: the variables, constraints, and pricing logic for a product family. Most teams start with their gnarliest line and expand from there.

Does it work with AI agents?

The engine is a full MCP server — connect it to Claude or any MCP-capable agent and your team quotes conversationally while every number still comes from your rules. Enterprise plans can run it inside your own cloud.
