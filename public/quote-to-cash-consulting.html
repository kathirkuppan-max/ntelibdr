In short: Nteli is a Salesforce Premier Partner that designs and implements quote-to-cash for specialty manufacturers: configure, price and quote in Salesforce, contracts and agreements, the order in your ERP, and invoicing, so every order and invoice traces back to the quote the customer accepted.

What quote-to-cash covers in a manufacturer

StepWhere it usually livesWhat goes wrong
ConfigureSalesforce (Revenue Cloud or CPQ)Options that can’t be built together get quoted; engineering finds out after the order.
PriceSalesforce or the ERPContract, dealer and volume prices kept in spreadsheets drift from the price book.
QuoteSalesforceQuotes leave without the right terms, lead times or approvals.
Contract and agreementsSalesforce (Manufacturing Cloud, CLM)Agreed volumes and prices aren’t applied to the next order.
OrderERPThe accepted quote is retyped into the ERP at a different price or configuration.
Invoice and revenueERP or Revenue Cloud BillingInvoices don’t match what was quoted; credits and rebates are paid by hand.

Quote-to-cash projects pay back at the handoffs. When the accepted quote becomes the ERP order automatically, and the invoice comes back linked to it, you can check deal by deal that what was quoted is what was ordered and billed.

How we run a quote-to-cash engagement

  1. Assess. Walk one real deal from request to invoice and mark where it’s retyped, re-priced or approved by email.
  2. Design. Decide which system owns each step: catalog, pricing, quote, agreement, order, invoice.
  3. Build in Salesforce. Revenue Cloud or CPQ for configured products, Manufacturing Cloud for agreements and forecasts, quote documents in your format.
  4. Connect the ERP. Accepted quote to ERP order, with order status and invoices coming back.
  5. Go live in phases. One product line or channel first, then the rest.
  6. Stay on. Managed-services hours each month for changes, new products and new price lists.

FAQ

What is quote-to-cash?

Quote-to-cash (Q2C) is the path from a customer’s request to cash in the bank: configure the product, price it, quote it, agree the contract, take the order, invoice and recognize the revenue. In a manufacturer it usually spans Salesforce and the ERP.

How is quote-to-cash different from CPQ?

CPQ (configure, price, quote) is the front of quote-to-cash. Q2C also covers what happens after the quote is accepted: contracts and agreements, the ERP order, invoicing and revenue. Most leakage happens at the handoffs between those steps.

Do we need Salesforce Revenue Cloud for quote-to-cash?

Not always. Revenue Cloud is Salesforce’s quote-to-cash product and the successor to Salesforce CPQ. Some manufacturers quote in Salesforce and bill in the ERP; others run billing in Revenue Cloud too. We design around which system should own each step.

How long does a quote-to-cash project take?

The first quote-to-order flow can go live in weeks. A full rollout across product lines, channels and the ERP takes longer and is phased. Scope depends on how configurable your products are, how pricing works and which ERP you run.

Which ERPs do you connect to?

We’ve connected Salesforce to SAP, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, Epicor and Infor in delivered projects. See our ERP integration guides.

Do you handle quoting through dealers and distributors?

Yes. Many of our manufacturing customers sell through dealers. Partner portals on Experience Cloud let dealers quote within your rules and place orders you can trace back to the quote.
