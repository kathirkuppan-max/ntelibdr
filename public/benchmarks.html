The Leak Ledger Method · Benchmarks

Revenue leakage benchmarks, with the math shown.

These are the rates Nteli's leak-ledger calculator uses to estimate how much revenue a company is leaking across the Quote-to-Comply pipeline — one set per revenue motion. Every estimate on this site shows its arithmetic; this page is where the arithmetic lives.

What these numbers are — and aren't. These are Nteli's benchmark planning rates, drawn from our engagement experience across manufacturing, channel and SaaS clients since 2019, and set deliberately conservative. They are not industry-survey statistics, and we don't present them as anyone else's research. They exist to produce a defensible starting estimate; a Leak Ledger Workshop replaces every rate on this page with your own data in one day.

Direct manufacturing — Quote → Order → Recognize → Comply

configure-to-order products sold direct · assumes ~40% of revenue flows through quotes

LeverBenchmark rateThe arithmetic
Quote3% config/discount error, 60% unrecoveredquoted value × 3% × 60% — configurations that can't be built as sold, discounts off policy, prices from a stale book.
Order2% of orders re-keyed wrong, $1,800 eachorders × 2% × $1,800 — the cost of an order that drifts from the quote between systems.
Recognize1.5% billed 45 days late + 0.3% never billedrevenue × 1.5% × (45/365) × 10% WACC + revenue × 0.3% — carrying cost of late billing, plus work that never reaches an invoice.
Comply0.15% tax exposurerevenue × 0.15% — amended returns and over-remitted tax.
