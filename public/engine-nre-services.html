However it arrives

The scope the standards add

The device class, sterility, patient contact and electronics decide which deliverables the quote must carry. Each one comes with its source, so nothing the customer will need later is left off.

03 · THE TERMS

Paid upfront, or recovered

Milestones that tie to the total, or a per-unit charge over the committed volume, with break-even, a stress case and the true-up clause on the quote.
