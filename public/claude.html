Connect it today

Same engine, same rules, same connector URL — pick the assistant your team already uses.

In Claude

  1. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector
  2. URL: https://quote.nteli.io/mcp
  3. Sign up with a work email at quote.nteli.io/signup — pick your vertical, and the starter pack loads into your workspace

In ChatGPT

  1. Settings → turn on Developer mode
  2. Add a custom connector with the URL https://quote.nteli.io/mcp
  3. Sign up with a work email at quote.nteli.io/signup — pick your vertical, and the starter pack loads into your workspace

Developer mode is needed to add a custom connector, so you need a ChatGPT plan that includes it.
