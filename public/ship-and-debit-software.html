One deal spine, from registration to credit memo.

The claim spans three systems that never talk: the agreement lives in the CRM, the transactions in the ERP, the claim in the distributor's file. Reconciliation joins them on one deal ID.

Design registration / SPA (CRM) → Order → Invoice (ERP) → EDI 867 POS / resale line → Claim → Match / Dispute → Credit memo (ERP) → GL

MatchEvery line to its agreement

Each claim line matched to the EDI 867 resale (POS) record by distributor, invoice, line, part, end customer and quantity — then to the design registration or SPA and the contract price, with duplicate detection. Line by line, not sampled after the quarter closes.

InventoryClaims against what the distributor holds

Debits checked against distributor inventory and authorized-debit quantities, so a claim cannot cover more units than were bought, held and resold under the agreement.

DisputeEvidence, not arguments

Invalid lines go back with the registration, price and POS record attached. The distributor relationship survives; the leak doesn't.

SettleAggregates burst to lines

The distributor still gets one credit memo per period. The auditor gets every line, traced to its deal.

GovernAgents run, people govern

Agents do the matching at claim volume. Rule changes are approved by a second person, versioned, and replayed on past claims before they ship.

StandaloneA module, not a migration

Runs beside what you have and accepts outside inputs — claims as files, POS feeds, ERP exports. No rip-and-replace.

ERPRead-mostly connection

It sees orders, invoices and credit memos; it rewrites nothing. The connection is scoped once as onboarding — not a monthly fee.
