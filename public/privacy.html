Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026 · Contact: hello@nteli.io

This policy describes how Nteli LLC ("Nteli", "we") handles personal information for visitors to nteli.io and users of the Nteli Agent Pricing Engine (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your work email address and name. Sign-up uses work email domains; a workspace is created for your company's domain.
  • Workspace content. Product models (rule packs), quotes, configuration sessions, approvals, and audit records your team creates in the Service. This content belongs to your company (see our Terms).
  • Usage data. Sessions, quotes submitted, and engine calls, used for billing (credits) and service operation.
  • Website analytics. On nteli.io we use Vercel Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and Google Analytics to understand site usage. These may use cookies or similar technologies.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and secure the Service, including authentication emails and workspace isolation.
  • To meter usage and bill credits (the billable unit is a submitted quote).
  • To respond to support requests at hello@nteli.io.
  • To improve the Service and website. We do not sell personal information.

3. AI and your data

The engine's pricing decisions are computed by a deterministic rules engine from your workspace's rule packs — not by a machine-learning model trained on your data. When you connect an AI assistant (for example Claude, via MCP), your conversation is processed by that assistant under its provider's terms; the engine receives the facts needed to configure and price a quote. We do not use your workspace content to train AI models.

4. Service providers

We use subprocessors to run the Service: Fly.io (application hosting), Resend (authentication and transactional email), Vercel (website hosting and analytics), Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics (website analytics), Calendly (scheduling). Each receives only the data needed for its function.

5. Retention and deletion

Workspace content is retained while your workspace is active. Workspace admins may request export or deletion of their workspace by emailing hello@nteli.io; we will complete deletion within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal or billing purposes. Audit trails within an active workspace are retained by design — that is the product.

6. Security

Workspaces are isolated per company domain with their own engine database. Access is authenticated via emailed sign-in links; rule changes and approvals are role-controlled and audit-logged.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information. Contact hello@nteli.io and we will respond within applicable legal timelines. For California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

8. International transfers

The Service is operated from the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States.

9. Changes

We will post updates to this page and revise the effective date. Material changes affecting workspace data will be announced to workspace admins by email.
