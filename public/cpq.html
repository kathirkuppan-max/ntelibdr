The Problem

Your CPQ is live. Your team isn't using it.

Most CPQ implementations take 12–18 months, go over budget, and end with reps exporting to Excel anyway. Revenue Cloud configurations that don't account for how billing and rev rec actually work just create more manual work downstream.

Without a working CPQ system

✕
Reps quote in PowerPoint or Excel — CPQ bypassed entirely, pricing errors compound, and unauthorized discounts only surface at billing
✕
Approval chains take days — deals stall at deal desk, discounts go unauthorised, and exception handling is tracked in email threads
✕
Billing runs off contract PDFs — not system-generated orders, so every invoice requires manual reference back to the signed contract
✕
Finance reconciles revenue manually each month against bank statements, with no system-of-record for what was contracted vs. what was billed
✕
ASC 606 compliance is a spreadsheet exercise at year-end — auditors request backup every quarter; CFO sweats the close

With Nteli CPQ — Day 31

✓
Guided selling leads reps to the right product and price every time — constraint rules, pricing waterfalls, and discount governance built for your sales motion
✓
Approval rules automated with escalation paths and SLA timers — deal desk notified on exceptions; mobile approvals for on-the-road sign-off
✓
Billing triggers fire automatically from contract execution events — amendment in CRM flows to invoice in hours, not at month-end
✓
Revenue recognition runs against SSP allocations set at order creation — multi-element arrangements split and recognised correctly from day one

CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) is the quoting engine — it manages product configuration, pricing rules, approval workflows, and quote generation. Revenue Cloud is the full quote-to-cash lifecycle: CPQ plus billing, revenue schedules, multi-element arrangement handling, and ASC 606-compliant revenue recognition. We implement both — and the integration between them — so your CPQ quotes flow automatically into billing and revenue recognition without manual handoffs.
