Regulatory tailwinds — 2025–2027

IRA Maximum Fair Price & 340B deduplication — CMS-negotiated drug prices took effect Jan 1, 2026. Manufacturers must identify, deduplicate, and document every covered-entity Medicare transaction. Nteli has built this into Rule R-08.
Multi-state tax nexus expansion — Economic nexus thresholds have brought 47 states into compliance scope for most mid-market distributors. Channel transaction tax is newly material for clients who previously filed in 3–5 states.
ASC 606 / IFRS 15 audit scrutiny — Revenue recognition in channel programs (sell-through, consignment, price protection) is under increasing audit pressure. Nteli's GL-level accrual architecture is purpose-built for this.
Salesforce Manufacturing Cloud adoption — Salesforce's push into manufacturing and distribution is creating demand for implementation partners who understand channel data, not just CRM configuration. Nteli is already certified and active here.
