“Customer wants a stairlift: curved stair with one landing, rider is 280 lb, it's for an outdoor porch at their home.”

What you’ll get: A draft at $15,350: a curved stairlift rated for the rider, a rail bent to the site survey, the outdoor package, installation and freight. It asks the one thing that moves the price, the nose-to-floor length, and prices every answer ($15,350 up to 10 ft, $20,950 at 30–34 ft, over 34 ft goes to Specials). Power swivel is listed to confirm with the customer.

“Wheelchair porch lift at a private home: about a 5 ft rise, goes up beside the porch, outdoors.”

What you’ll get: A draft at $13,700, checked against ASME A18.1 for a home. Two questions come back, each priced: a third landing means a fully enclosed runway ($22,400), and a load over 750 lb goes to Specials. The dedicated 120 V supply and disconnect are listed as by others.

“Customer wants an interior scooter lift for their sedan. The scooter weighs about 220 lb.”

What you’ll get: No quote for something that won’t fit. A car’s trunk takes no interior lift, so it offers the two ways out with prices: an exterior lift on the hitch from $2,675 (hitch added if needed), or an interior lift if it’s really going on a crossover or SUV, from $3,875.

01 · THE REQUEST

However it arrives

A phone note, an email from a family member, a therapist’s referral. Paste it as written; the engine pulls out what it needs.

02 · THE CHECK

Fit and code, before the visit

Rider weight against the lift’s rating, stair shape against the rail, rise against what a platform lift may travel, the vehicle against the lift. Anything that won’t work comes back with the ways out, not a wrong price.

03 · THE QUESTIONS

Only the ones that move the price

Nose-to-floor length, number of landings, the hitch on the vehicle. Each comes with how to measure it and what each answer costs, so the home visit confirms the quote instead of starting it.

Built for the dealer’s desk. Quotes carry your prices, your freight zone and your install rates once your price book loads. Jobs beyond standard (over 14 ft of rise, an over-weight platform, a stair over 34 ft) are flagged for Specials with the reason, so nothing is promised that the factory won’t build.
