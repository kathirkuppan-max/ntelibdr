The Leak Ledger Method

Where Quote-to-Order leaks.

Most of the money is lost at the quote and the order. What follows the order, recognizing the revenue and filing the tax right, is where those errors surface later. Illustrative numbers for a $200M manufacturer — compute yours in 30 seconds.

LEVER 01

Quote

Configuration errors and unapproved discounts leak margin on deals you win.

~$1.4M / yr
LEVER 02

Order

The handoff re-keys into the ERP what sales already closed.

~$220K / yr
LEVER 03

Recognize

Late billing, Excel rev rec, channel claims posted unvalidated.

~$600K–1.2M / yr
LEVER 04

Comply

Tax filed on pre-claim numbers; exemption certs expire silently.

~$150–400K / yr
