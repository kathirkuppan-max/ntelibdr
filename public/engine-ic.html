Instrumentation and controls — wetted materials, hazardous-area protection and safety integrity checked before pricing.

What it quotes

Pressure and DP transmitters, temperature assemblies (sensor, transmitter, thermowell), radar level, magnetic flowmeters, control valves with digital positioners and pH analyzers — on 4-20 mA HART, FOUNDATION Fieldbus or PROFIBUS PA, one tag or a hundred.

What it checks

Wetted materials for the process, including sour service to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156; hazardous-area protection to NEC 500/505 and IEC 60079; valve body rating to ASME B16.34 and seat leakage to FCI 70-2; and safety integrity: SIL loops get IEC 61508-certified devices.

On every quote

The certificates the order needs (NACE declaration, EN 10204 3.1, calibration) priced as lines, an instrument data sheet per tag, and the facts the customer must confirm — H2S partial pressure, gas group, valve sizing — stated rather than buried.

NACE / ISO 15156

Sour-service material limits are revised by edition, and the declaration the customer expects follows the edition.

Hazardous areas

NEC 500/505 and IEC 60079 move on their own cycles, and the approval marking on each device has to match the area it mounts in.

Functional safety

IEC 61508 and 61511 decide which devices may sit in a safety loop, and which certificates come with them.

Ordering codes

Makers revise model codes and options every year; a quote built on last year’s code is a reorder waiting to happen.

THE REFUSAL
“A pressure transmitter on FOUNDATION Fieldbus for a SIL 2 safety loop.”

What you’ll see: “FOUNDATION Fieldbus and SIL 2 can’t go together”: SIL-certified devices (IEC 61508) are offered with a 4-20 mA HART output, and fieldbus versions carry no SIL certificate. Two ways out, each priced: HART for this loop with the SIL certificate, from about $1,785 (the usual choice), or keep the fieldbus if it isn’t a safety loop, from about $1,575.

“Need pricing on 6 pressure transmitters for a gas compressor skid. Max 1,200 psig, process around 40 °C. The gas has some H2S so NACE is required. Class I Div 1 Group D. 4-20 mA HART.”

What you’ll see: An explosionproof Div 1 housing, wetted parts and bolting to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 with the declaration and EN 10204 3.1 certificates, and per-device configuration and calibration — about $9,400 for six with the volume break. Better and Best step up to intrinsically safe housings and tantalum diaphragms, and the quote asks you to confirm the H2S partial pressure and gas group.

“Quote a 4 in control valve on steam, 580 psi max, 390 °F, fail closed, Class V shutoff, 4-20 mA HART, general purpose area.”

What you’ll see: A globe valve with a spring-return diaphragm actuator, a Class 600 body rated for the pressure at temperature (ASME B16.34), a lapped metal seat with the Class V leakage test (FCI 70-2) and a digital HART positioner — about $9,600, with the Cv sizing listed for the customer to confirm.
