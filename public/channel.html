📖 Revenue recognition policy · ASC 606 sell-through model
Performance obligation: transfer of control to the OEM end-customer (not the distributor). Distributors operate under stock-rotation and price-protection rights — these are variable consideration, not separate POs.

Recognition trigger: POS line received from distributor (EDI 867) and matched to PUI bucket. Until then, revenue is deferred (account 2100).

Variable consideration estimate: S&D, price protection, stock rotation, and volume rebates are accrued at sell-in (JE-001) using expected-value method, true-up at each period close. Constrained when uncertainty is high (new distributor, EOL approaching).

Returns / stock rotation reserve: recognized as contra-revenue with reserve liability (2220) — reverses on RMA via JE-008.

Significant judgments disclosed: (a) point-in-time recognition on POS event, not period-end; (b) FX rate locked at DR creation with quarterly true-up; (c) MDF treated as marketing expense not contra-revenue (policy choice, override per program available).
