FAQ

What is Salesforce Manufacturing Cloud?

Salesforce’s product for manufacturers’ run-rate business: sales agreements with planned volumes and prices, account-based forecasting, and rebate management, on top of Sales Cloud.

Do we need Manufacturing Cloud if we already have Sales Cloud?

If much of your revenue comes from recurring orders under agreements, forecasts by account and product, or rebate programs, yes. If you mostly sell one-off configured projects, Revenue Cloud or CPQ may matter more. Many manufacturers use both.

What&rsquo;s the difference between Manufacturing Cloud and Revenue Cloud?

Manufacturing Cloud manages agreements, forecasts and rebates. Revenue Cloud handles the transaction: configuring, pricing, quoting, ordering and billing. Our Salesforce for manufacturers page compares them.

Where does the actuals data come from?

From your ERP. Orders and shipments flow into Salesforce so agreements show planned versus actual and forecasts start from real history. We’ve connected Salesforce to SAP, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, Epicor and Infor.

How long does a Manufacturing Cloud implementation take?

A first phase, sales agreements and account-based forecasting for one business unit with ERP actuals, can go live in weeks to a few months. Rebates, partner visibility and more business units follow.

Who sells the Manufacturing Cloud licenses?

Salesforce. We implement, and we work alongside your Salesforce account executive so licensing and services line up.
