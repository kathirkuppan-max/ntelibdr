## How to measure it
- **Signups:** `quote.nteli.io/signup?src=slack-splash`. Count them in the engine's usage report by src.
- **Demo-page visits:** `nteli.io/engine?utm_source=slack&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=splash-test`, read in Vercel Web Analytics by referrer and UTM.
- **ChatGPT installs:** no tracking on the listing link itself. Read installs from new ChatGPT-origin sessions in the engine around the post date.
- **Replies and DMs in the Slack channel:** log them below by hand.
