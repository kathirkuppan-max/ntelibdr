# Leak Ledger video: voiceover scripts

Two cuts, both rendered from `leak-ledger.html`: `leak-ledger.mp4` (79 s) and `leak-ledger-60s.mp4` (60 s).
Voiced versions: `leak-ledger-vo.mp4` and `leak-ledger-60s-vo.mp4`, spoken by macOS "Ava (Premium)" via `python3 voiceover.py`
(the lines live in that script; edit there and re-run). "Nteli" is spoken as "En-telly" (confirmed by Kathir, 2026-10-30).
Read at about 140 words a minute, calm and plain, CFO register. Timecodes match the picture, so each line starts on its
scene. Leave the music bed under the gaps.

Rules for any rewrite: figures are illustrative (say "a sample company" or "in this example", never a customer);
no speed words (minutes, instantly, real-time); never say the engine detects rule changes on its own; never mention rule
counts, models or how the engine works inside.

Hold before outreach: the video shows the finished stack. The Salesforce, SyteLine and NetSuite connectors and most of
the modules aren't live yet. Until they are, either hold it or add "here's where we're taking it" to the send.
