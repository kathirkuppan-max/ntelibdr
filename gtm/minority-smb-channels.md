Tier-1 notes:
- **HUB is the master key.** Every Texas state agency and public university carries
  statutory HUB spend goals (professional services 23.7%, other services 26%,
  commodities 21.1%) and good-faith-effort requirements under Gov't Code ch. 2161.
  Certification lists Nteli in the statewide HUB Directory that agency and
  university buyers are told to search first. H-E-B's corporate program accepts
  it too. Docs needed: LLC formation docs, operating agreement, owner ID/proof
  of residency and group membership, tax returns, sworn application.
- **HUB application can piggyback Austin MBE**: SMBR forwards MBE applications to
  the Comptroller for HUB consideration — one packet, two certs. Decide direction
  (HUB-first vs. Austin-first) when assembling docs; HUB-first is simpler if city
  work isn't imminent.
- **SAM.gov**: the **DUNS (117298094) is no longer the federal ID** — DUNS was
  retired April 2022, replaced by the 12-char UEI issued free inside SAM.gov.
  Registration needs: legal name/address exactly matching IRS records, EIN,
  bank info (for payments), NAICS codes (541511, 541512, 541611, 513210),
  and annual renewal. Also the prerequisite for 8(a) and for bidding anything federal.
- **SDB self-cert** counts for federal SDB set-aside goals and many corporate
  supplier-diversity intake forms — zero paperwork beyond SAM. Eligibility
  confirmed by Kathir 2026-11-02; with 50/50 ownership the attestation rests on
  *both* owners meeting the disadvantage tests, so both review it before the
  box is checked.

Tier-2 notes:
- **NMSDC is the corporate-door key** (Tier 4 below mostly requires it or
  equivalent). It's the only paid cert on this list. Trigger: the first corporate
  supplier-diversity program worth entering, or a prospect that asks. The NMSDC
  Certification Reimbursement Initiative may make it net-free via a sponsoring
  corporate member.
- **8(a) caution (2026)**: SBA's June 2026 proposed rule would remove the
  rebuttable presumption of social disadvantage for individually-owned firms —
  new applicants would need an individualized social-disadvantage narrative.
  Rule not final; the program's shape for new applicants is in flux. 8(a) is the
  single most valuable federal credential (sole-source awards up to ~$4.5M for
  services) but also the most paperwork and a one-shot 9-year clock — don't start
  it before there's a federal pipeline to justify it. Revisit Q1 2027 or when a
  federal prime/agency shows interest.
- **DBE** only matters for federally-funded transportation buyers (CapMetro,
  airport, TxDOT itself). They do buy software/IT occasionally, but this is the
  weakest fit — do it only if a specific CapMetro/airport solicitation appears.
