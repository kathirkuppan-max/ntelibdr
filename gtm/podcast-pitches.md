## Paste-ready short pitch (forms / LinkedIn) — adjust the episode idea per show

I'd like to be a guest. I spent ten years building product configurators and pricing systems
at Cisco, Dell and IBM, and now run Nteli in Austin, a Salesforce Premier Partner that builds
quoting software for configure-to-order manufacturers.

Episode idea: **[episode idea from the table]**. My argument: revenue operations built a
discipline around the pipeline and left everything after the signature unaudited. The CRM,
ERP, billing and channel systems each keep their own version of a deal, and the money leaks
in the gaps between them: discounts that run deeper than peer deals, orders entered at a
different price than quoted, invoices off contract, distributor claims paid unchecked. I'd
walk through where those leaks hide and the checks that catch them before the money moves.
