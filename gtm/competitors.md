# Competitor notes

## CostBook (costbook.app) — evaluated 2026-11-03
**What:** spreadsheet-replacement quoting for UL 508A panel shops and custom builders
(test fixtures, integrators, custom equipment, IC test hardware, switchboards).
Solo founder (Cloyd Abad, cloyd.abad@costbook.app). $99/mo Base (3 quote builders) /
$199/mo Pro (unlimited + QBO export), per business. **Founding program: first 10 shops
at $75/mo locked 12 months** — same stage as us, same first customers, moving now.

**Mechanism:** price book + BOM import (EPLAN / AutoCAD Electrical / Excel) →
completeness checklist (FAT, crate, UL label, wiring hours required on every quote) →
price-change alerts recheck open quotes (copper/breaker moves → margin hit shown) →
quote link/PDF, customer accepts online. Good estimating hygiene; the BOM-import wedge
is smart.

**What it is NOT:** no rules engine, no compliance checking (no SCCR arithmetic, no
NEC/UL validation), no configurator, no AI intake, no channel/order/invoice story.
**Their quote can be complete and still describe a panel that fails inspection.**
Completeness (theirs) vs correctness (ours) is the differentiation line.

**SEO:** took #1 on "ul 508a quoting software" within ~2 weeks of publishing a dedicated
commercial landing page (dated 2026-10-19). Lesson: "[x] software" queries reward product
pages over guides. Response: put the term on /engine/panels (title/meta) + interlink with
the UL 508A guide; backlinks.

**Threat:** real, narrow. Wins "replace my spreadsheet" at the small end. Their homepage
narrative (quoted 24% → built 14.5%, "no single mistake to blame") is a leak-ledger story —
they educate the market in our direction. CostBook-equipped shops that still lose jobs to
misconfiguration are qualified leads for the engine.

**Actions:** (1) engine-panels on-page SEO for the lane term; (2) "complete ≠ correct"
line in panels-vertical copy and outreach; (3) outbound speed — panel-shop batch races
their founding program.
